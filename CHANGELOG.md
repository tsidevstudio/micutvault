# MicutVault Changelog

This changelog summarizes public, user-facing MicutVault product changes.

Internal maintenance, private implementation changes and security-sensitive details may be intentionally omitted.

## 1.2.0 — November 4, 2026

### First Public Release

MicutVault is now publicly available on Google Play after completing the required closed-testing period.

### Private vault

- Private storage for photos
- Private storage for videos
- Private audio storage
- Private notes
- Primary PIN-protected access
- Optional biometric unlock

### Disguise system

Added 9 disguise modes:

- Calculator
- Notes
- Books
- Files
- Calendar
- Clock
- Tasks
- Scanner
- Recorder

Calculator mode also functions as a real calculator while supporting private PIN access to the vault.

### Privacy and security

- Configurable inactivity auto-lock
- Optional intruder detection
- Configurable lockout protection after repeated failed PIN attempts
- 30-day encrypted Trash
- Encrypted backup and restore
- Local-first privacy model
- No account required
- No ads
- No analytics
- No mandatory cloud
- No subscription

### Media experience

- Photo zoom
- Sorting options
- Slideshow support
- Local browsing of protected content

### Google Play

MicutVault completed Google Play closed testing with more than 12 testers over the required 14-day period and received Production access.

The app became publicly available on Google Play on **November 4, 2026**.

### Current availability

The current Production release is fully available for free.

A future update is planned to introduce a **Free + Lifetime** model with a one-time purchase.

No subscription is planned.
